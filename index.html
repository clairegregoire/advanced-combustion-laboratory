Dr. Claire Grégoire

Assistant Professor

Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering
University of Florida

Dr. Grégoire’s research focuses on the experimental investigation of reactive chemical systems through laser absorption spectroscopy, optical diagnostics, and detailed chemical kinetic modeling.

Her work aims to advance the fundamental understanding of combustion processes relevant to sustainable energy technologies, next-generation lithium-ion battery safety, and advanced propulsion systems.

She specializes in shock-tube experiments and time-resolved species measurements to elucidate reaction pathways under extreme conditions, including those relevant to emerging fuels and solid rocket propellants.
