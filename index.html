Dr. Claire Grégoire

Assistant Professor

Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering
University of Florida

Dr. Grégoire’s research focuses on the experimental and computational investigation of reactive chemical systems, combining laser absorption spectroscopy, advanced analytical methods (GC/MS and FTIR), and detailed chemical kinetic modeling. Her work advances the understanding of high-temperature reaction kinetics and combustion phenomena under extreme conditions, with applications in energy conversion, lithium-ion battery safety, and advanced propulsion, including emerging fuels and solid rocket propellants.
