Welcome

Welcome to the Advanced Combustion Laboratory at the University of Florida, directed by Dr. Claire Grégoire.

Our laboratory is dedicated to advancing fundamental understanding and predictive capabilities in combustion science. Through research and scientific collaboration, we aim to contribute to the development of improved combustion technologies and a deeper understanding of complex reacting flows.
